The IMU axis calibrator has been retired

This page relabelled sensor axes by hand, against remap names and a hard-coded bridge address that no longer exist. Calibration now follows the motion pipeline (software/MOTION_PIPELINE.md, section 3):

  • Neutral capture, every power-up: forearm pointing at the screen, palm down, wrist straight, fingers extended, hold still. Start it from the prompt on the Operator stage, the IMU bench, the device crown, or {"cmd":"calibrate","what":"neutral"}.
  • Wrist axis (optional, persisted): 5 s of wrist flexion/extension measures the hand sensor's true flexion axis. Same places, "what":"wrist_axis".
  • Mounting (remap, offsets, the 4-tap alignment) lives on the IMU bench, where every change applies on the next frame and persists in the bridge.
