1. Controller

Juan Sebastian Molano (contact details as in the Impressum above) is the data controller (Art. 4(7) GDPR) for this website.

2. The short version

  • No cookies. This site sets no cookies of any kind.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads. No visitor profiles, no fingerprinting, no third-party analytics.
  • No third-party content. All fonts, scripts, styles, and 3D assets are served from this site itself; your browser contacts no other server when you view it.
  • No accounts, no forms. The site collects nothing you type; the only way to send us data is to write an e-mail yourself.

3. Server logs (hosting)

When the site is retrieved, the hosting provider technically processes the data your browser transmits (IP address, date and time, requested resource, user agent) in server log files. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in securely operating and delivering the website). Logs are kept only as long as the provider's standard retention requires and are not merged with other data. [Hosting provider to be named here once the site is deployed]

4. Local storage (functional only)

The console surfaces remember small functional settings (for example, which console you last opened) in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or anyone else, contains no personal data, and you can clear it at any time via your browser (site data → clear). As strictly functional storage it requires no consent banner (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG).

5. Live device data

When you connect the console to a TAKTO device or its simulator, all telemetry is exchanged between your browser and your own host machine on your own network. It is not sent to us, stored by us, or shared with anyone. Data protection by design (Art. 25 GDPR) is an architecture decision of this project: recordings live on the device's SD card and your own computer only.

6. Contact by e-mail

If you write to us, we process your e-mail address and message content to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Correspondence about job or research opportunities is kept only as long as the conversation requires, then deleted on request or when no longer needed.

7. Downloads

The documents offered for download (thesis, paper, CAD release) are static files; downloading them transmits no personal data beyond the server log described above.
